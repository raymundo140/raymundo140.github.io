## Overview

At Smart Factory MTY, I worked directly with industrial and collaborative robot platforms in a manufacturing-focused lab. My work covered assembly, commissioning, fault diagnosis, maintenance, calibration, mechanical fabrication, end-effector development, and production-cell testing.

## Robot Platforms

- Universal Robots UR3
- Universal Robots UR5
- xArm 5
- xArm 6
- Rethink Robotics Baxter
- ABB YuMi

## Commissioning & Maintenance

- Assembled and integrated robot platforms and peripherals.
- Diagnosed electrical and mechanical faults.
- Performed corrective repairs and preventive maintenance.
- Configured tool-center points and robot calibration.
- Validated reach, repeatability, and cycle behavior in production cells.

**Impact:** Maintenance and root-cause work helped reduce robot downtime by 20%.

## Custom Gripper

I designed and fabricated a high-precision end effector for pick-and-place work.

- SolidWorks mechanical design.
- Iteration of finger geometry and mounting interfaces.
- 3D-printed prototype components.
- Laser-cut structural parts.
- Physical fit testing.
- Production-cell validation.

**Impact:** The final gripper improved handling repeatability and increased throughput by 30%.

## Additional Engineering

I also designed and fabricated brackets, mounts, enclosures, tool holders, and other robot-cell components using SolidWorks, 3D printing, and laser cutting.
