## Overview

Led a team building a scale autonomous tractor for a John Deere navigation challenge. The system estimated vehicle position from camera, encoder, and IMU feedback, received target coordinates wirelessly, and used closed-loop control to drive autonomously toward each target.

## Embedded Platform

The tractor was built around an STM32H755 dual-core microcontroller with supporting sensors, motor/steering control, and wireless communication.

The platform integrated:
- DC traction motor with encoder feedback.
- Steering servo.
- IMU feedback.
- Wireless target-coordinate reception.
- CAN, SPI, I2C, and UART communication.
- PWM motor and steering control.

## My Contribution — Navigation

The embedded software used non-blocking state-machine logic to coordinate navigation, waypoint updates, motor commands, steering, and communication.

My work included team leadership, hardware integration, peripheral configuration, embedded communication, PWM/SPI implementation, and full-system integration.

## Result

The final prototype autonomously estimated its state and drove toward externally provided target coordinates on the physical scale tractor.
