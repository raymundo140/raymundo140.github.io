## Overview

Built an autonomous visual-tracking system for a DJI Tello that recognizes a registered person and controls the drone to keep that target centered and at a roughly consistent distance.

## Perception

A reference face is encoded at startup. During flight, camera frames are processed and compared against the registered target.

To keep the system responsive:
- Recognition runs periodically rather than on every frame.
- Frames are downscaled for face processing.
- A background thread continuously acquires the newest video frame.

## Control

The target's position in the image is converted directly into motion commands:
- Horizontal error controls yaw.
- Vertical error controls altitude.
- Apparent face size approximates distance for forward/backward motion.

Command limits reduce abrupt motion, and a fail-safe hover is triggered when the registered target is lost.

## Result

The system closed the full perception-to-action loop on a physical UAV and maintained the target near the center of the image while adjusting orientation, height, and distance in real time.
