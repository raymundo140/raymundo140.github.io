## Overview

Designed and built a five-floor, dual-cabin elevator prototype at the University of Seoul. The system combines mechanical design, embedded control, user input, actuation, safety sensing, and request scheduling on an STM32 NUCLEO-G474RE.

## Architecture

Each elevator runs through independent non-blocking state logic. Requests are entered through a 4x4 keypad and stored in a shared queue, while a scheduler assigns requests to the most suitable cabin.

## My Contribution — Scheduling

A deterministic greedy scheduler evaluates:
- Distance to the pickup floor.
- Cabin availability.
- Directional preference.

The selected elevator then executes the pickup and destination sequence through its own finite-state machine.

## Hardware

- STM32 NUCLEO-G474RE.
- Two continuous-rotation servos.
- 4x4 matrix keypad.
- Hall-effect / magnetic safety sensing.
- Hardware PWM.
- UART debugging/monitoring.
- SolidWorks mechanical design.
- 3D-printed structure and cabins.

## Result

The final system operated both cabins independently while sharing request and scheduling logic, demonstrating real-time embedded control and complete hardware–software integration in a physical prototype.
