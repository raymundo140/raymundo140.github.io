## About This Work

Conducted research on solid-state device physics, connecting neuromorphic and
memristor-based capabilities to energy-efficient intelligent robotics. Invited by
the engineering faculty to deliver a lecture to an international cohort at the
University of Seoul on neuromorphic architectures and hardware-accelerated AI
systems.
